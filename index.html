[Full text analysis placeholder...] Enterprise risk management (ERM) in the public sector involves identifying, analyzing, and responding to risk factors throughout the life of a government program. Proper implementation ensures that institutional objectives are met with efficiency and transparency.

By establishing rigorous internal controls and conducting regular value-for-money assessments, ministries can proactively prevent fiscal leakages, ensuring taxpayer dollars are maximized to serve the public interest effectively.
